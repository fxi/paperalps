# Changes

What changed for visitors of https://fxi.io/paperalps/, by release. Newest
first. The details are in the commits.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-11)

First public release.

- **The 50 summits.** A wordless escape game drawn over the map, for curious
  experts. The first five steps are open; the chain stops after the fifth
  for now. Progress is saved in the browser.
- **A toolbox**, available from the start: a cipher machine, a notepad, a
  bit grid, a GIS with a plan view, a recorder that can slow, speed up and
  reverse sound, and a small code sandbox.
- **Night**, with lamps on the terrain, and a quieter score that changes mood
  with the game.
- One set of icons, the same on every system.
- **Flight**: aircraft-style sticks on touch, a sonic variometer, a camera that
  looks at the horizon with roll, click a summit or a city to be flown there.
- **Map**: city labels, a minimap toggle, a north button that resets the
  heading, steadier peak labels.
- **Look**: paper and ink shading over a 2.5D terrain of the Alps, a dark drone
  score.
